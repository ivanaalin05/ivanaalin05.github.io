ABOUT ME

I'm Ivana Alin, a print operator and graphic designer with hands-on experience across the full production pipeline — from first concept to the final printed piece coming off the press.

My work spans logo design, digital illustration, poster design and visual compositions. I bring a creative instinct shaped by real print knowledge — understanding color profiles, resolution, bleeds and how a design must be built to survive production.

I work across CorelDraw, Adobe Photoshop and Adobe Illustrator, creating work that is both visually distinctive and technically sound. Whether it's a brand mark, a conceptual illustration or a print-ready layout — I deliver work that looks just as good in hand as it does on screen.
